Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy describes the "Leo Factory" application operated by Scarf and Bolts (the "App"). The App is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Scarf and Bolts YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

What the App accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the App uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's basic information (its name) to confirm the correct channel is connected. The App does not access, collect or store any information about viewers, other YouTube users or members of the public.

How information is stored

The only credential the App keeps is the channel owner's OAuth refresh token, stored encrypted in the owner's private GitHub repository secrets. It is used solely to upload the owner's videos and is never sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Third-party services

By using YouTube features of this App you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted.

Children

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message through the Scarf and Bolts YouTube channel.
