Scarf and Bolts

Short animated stories about Leo, a little robot with an orange scarf, learning what it means to be human.

New episodes every week: gentle comedy and warm endings for the whole family. Leo, his grumpy cat Mochi, his best friend Mia and the kind shopkeeper Mr. Rao get into a new small adventure in every short.

How it's made

Stories are written and directed by our small team and produced with the help of AI image, video and voice tools. Videos are labelled as AI-generated content on YouTube and Instagram.

Leo Factory: our publishing tool

Our internal tool, Leo Factory, uses YouTube API Services to upload our own approved videos to the Scarf and Bolts YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner and collects no data about viewers. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service; YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
